# OncoSense Watch States

The Watch experience uses plain-language screening states instead of unexplained numeric scores.

## 🟢 LOW SIGNAL

The current longitudinal pattern is stable relative to the user's baseline.

Example:
> Your health pattern is stable.

## 🟡 WATCH

A persistent change is emerging and should continue to be monitored.

Example:
> A health pattern has changed. OncoSense is continuing to monitor it.

## 🟠 EARLY SIGNAL

A persistent multi-signal pattern has crossed the research model's investigation threshold.

Example:
> A persistent pattern has changed. View the analysis on your iPhone.

## 🔴 HIGH SIGNAL

The research model identifies a strong disease-associated pattern and recommends prompt clinical evaluation.

Example:
> A significant health pattern was detected. Further medical evaluation is recommended.

These labels describe model output for screening/research. They are not a diagnosis by themselves. The detailed clinical context belongs on the iPhone experience.
